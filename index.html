Refinishing & Sanding

Bring your original floor back

Drag the slider to see a room before and after refinishing. Solid hardwood can be sanded back to fresh wood several times over its life. We sand in passes with finer grit each time so it comes out flat with no swirl marks.

  1. 36GRITStripTakes off the old finish, stain and cupping.
  2. 60GRITFlattenRemoves the scratches from the first pass.
  3. 100GRITFinish sandReady for stain, with edges and corners done by hand.
  4. 3×COATSStain & sealYour color, then three coats of oil- or water-based polyurethane.
Installation

Hardwood installation

Solid, engineered, LVP/LVT and laminate. We check the slab or subfloor for level and moisture first, and acclimate wood in your home before it goes down.

  • Solid & engineered hardwood
  • LVP / LVT and laminate
  • Stair treads & nosings

Repairs & Stain Matching

Repairs that disappear

We weave new boards into the existing floor and mix stain on site against your boards, so a repair or patch blends in with the rest of the room.

  • Water-damaged & pet-stained boards
  • Squeaks, gaps & cupping
  • Patching after wall removal
  • Thresholds & transitions

How it works

Five steps, no surprises

    Walkthrough
